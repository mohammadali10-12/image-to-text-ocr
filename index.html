    How it works

    1. 1

      Upload

      Drop, browse or paste one or more images. Rotate them if the text is sideways and pick the language of the text.

    2. 2

      Extract

      Tesseract OCR runs in your browser and reads the text. The first run downloads the OCR engine and language data, so it takes a little longer.

    3. 3

      Download

      Review and fix the text in the editor, then copy it to your clipboard or download it as a plain .txt file.

    Supported formats

    • JPG / JPEG
    • PNG
    • WebP
    • BMP
    • GIF first frame
    • TIFF first page, converted in your browser

    Maximum 10 MB per image and 10 images at a time. Very large images are scaled down to 4,000 px on the longest side before OCR to keep your browser responsive.

    OCR limitations

    • Works best on clear, printed text with good contrast and at least ~20 px tall letters.
    • Handwriting, decorative fonts, blurry photos and low-resolution screenshots often produce errors.
    • Choose the language that matches the text. Mixed-language images may need a separate pass for each language.
    • Complex layouts (tables, multiple columns) may not keep their original structure.
    • Tip: crop out unrelated parts of the image and keep the text level for better results.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. Your images and the extracted text are processed by JavaScript inside your browser and are never sent to our server or any OCR service. The only network downloads are the open-source OCR engine and language files, which are fetched from the jsDelivr CDN.

    Why is the first extraction slower?

    The first time you use a language, your browser downloads the OCR engine (a few MB) and the trained data for that language (roughly 1–15 MB depending on the language). Your browser caches these files, so later runs start faster.

    Which languages are supported?

    English, Hindi, Gujarati, Urdu, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. Language data is only downloaded when you choose that language.

    Can I extract text from several images at once?

    Yes. Add up to 10 images and they are processed one after another. The text from each image is combined into a single result, with the file name above each section.

    Does it work offline?

    Not reliably. The page and OCR files must be downloaded at least once, and offline use has not been tested, so please stay online while using the converter.

    Is it free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no API key and no usage limit beyond what your device can handle.

    About

    ImageToText OCR is a small, privacy-friendly tool for turning pictures of text into editable text. It is a static website built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and uses the open-source Tesseract.js engine for optical character recognition.

    There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers. Your theme choice is the only thing saved, in your own browser.

    Privacy: images and text never leave your device. The OCR engine and language data are downloaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net when needed.
