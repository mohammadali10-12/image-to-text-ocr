Contact

Found a bug, have a suggestion, or need help with the converter? Here is how to reach the maintainer.

Report a problem or ask a question

Please open an issue on the project’s GitHub page:

Open GitHub issues

A free GitHub account is required to post. Issues are public, so do not attach images that contain personal or confidential information. If an image triggers a bug, try to reproduce it with a harmless sample image instead.

Helpful details to include

  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • Your browser and device (for example, Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone).
  • The OCR language you selected and the image format and size.
  • Any error message shown on the page.

Privacy questions

The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is and isn’t collected. Because images are processed in your browser, the maintainer cannot see or recover any image or text you used on this site.

ImageToText OCR

Privacy: images and extracted text are processed in your browser and are never uploaded. The OCR engine and language data are downloaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net when needed.
